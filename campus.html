Academic Knowledge Hub

Central Digital Library

Our 3-story Central Library houses over 85,000 physical volumes, subscriptions to leading IEEE and Springer journals, and a 24/7 Digital Resource Hub equipped with high-speed internet work terminals.

  • Quiet study pods and collaborative conference rooms
  • E-book search workstation terminals
  • Automated RFID book circulation system
Interactive Learning

Smart Classrooms & Labs

Every lecture hall is equipped with high-definition digital smart boards, acoustic audio systems, and ergonomic seating to enhance interactive learning.

  • Climate-controlled air-conditioned rooms
  • High-speed Wi-Fi coverage across all blocks
  • Dedicated software and hardware engineering laboratories
Events & Conventions

Grand Auditorium & Media Hall

A 1,200-seat state-of-the-art auditorium equipped with surround sound, professional stage lighting, and broadcast streaming facilities for national conferences, cultural fests, and guest lectures.

Fitness & Athletics

Sports Complex & Gymnasium

Comprehensive athletic complex featuring synthetic basketball courts, a football ground, badminton courts, table tennis arenas, and a fully equipped fitness gym under expert trainers.

Dining & Refreshments

Campus Cafeteria & Food Court

Hygienic multi-cuisine food court serving nutritious meals, fresh snacks, coffee, and beverages prepared under strict health and sanitation guidelines.

Residential Life

Student Hostels & Residences

Separate secure residential hostels for male and female students with 24/7 security CCTV monitoring, resident wardens, study lounges, and high-speed fiber internet.
